Modern CSS Element Shapes

Examples of shaping elements with border-radius, corner-shape, border-shape, rounded polygon(), and shape(), including shadow behavior, progressive enhancement with @supports, and pointer hit-testing.

    1. Circles, Ellipses & Pills

    Proportional curves with aspect-ratio, elliptical border-radius (x / y), and organic radii without extra elements.

    2. Corner Geometry (corner-shape)

    Switch corner geometry with pure CSS :has(:checked). Borders, shadows, and outlines automatically follow the corners.

    Choose a corner shape

    3. Box Geometry (border-shape)

    border-shape reshapes the border, shadow, and focus ring directly using shape() or polygon(), falling back to clip-path + drop-shadow().

    4. Rounded Polygons (polygon(round ...))

    Soften polygon vertices with polygon(round <length>, ...) inside clip-path, paired with filter: drop-shadow() on a wrapper.

    5. Complex Paths & Soft Masks

    Use responsive shape() curves for tickets and cutouts, or mask-image when an edge needs alpha feathering.

    6. Shaped Hit-Testing & Focus

    Click inside the dashed bounding box vs. inside the diamond. Clipped corners outside the shape ignore clicks and pass them to the box behind.

    Click the diamond or a corner of the dashed box.
