Location-Agnostic Data

Demonstrate how Temporal.PlainDate and Temporal.PlainTime prevent "timezone drift" bugs by recording chronological data that remains identical regardless of the viewer's location.

🚫
This demo requires a browser with native Temporal support

Discrepancy: A Date is a timestamp, not a calendar date. Saving "1990-01-01" as a Date forces the app to pick an instant (midnight UTC here), so viewers west of UTC (e.g., New York) read it back as Dec 31, 1989. Temporal.PlainDate has no concept of time or zone, staying exact.
