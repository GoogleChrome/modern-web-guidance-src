Keyboard guidance

Focus a top-level trigger to preview its menu; press Enter or Space to dismiss the preview, or Down Arrow to enter it. In an open top-level menu, Left and Right Arrow switch to the adjacent menu and enter its first command. Use Up and Down to move between commands, Right Arrow to open Preferences, Left Arrow or Escape to return from the submenu, Right Arrow on a submenu item without a child submenu to preview the next top-level menu, and Tab to leave the menu.

Testing guidance

Test this demo with keyboard-only input, VoiceOver, and NVDA. The visible focus indicator and status message make the result easy to inspect.
