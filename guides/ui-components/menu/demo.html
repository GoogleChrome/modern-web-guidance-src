Keyboard guidance

Focus a top-level trigger to preview its menu; press Enter or Space to dismiss the preview, or Down Arrow to enter it, or Home or End to enter at the first or last command. In an open top-level menu, Left and Right Arrow preview the adjacent menu with focus on its trigger. Use Up and Down to move between commands, Right Arrow to open a submenu such as Export, Left Arrow or Escape to return from the submenu, Right Arrow on a submenu item without a child submenu to preview the next top-level menu, and Tab to leave the menu.

Testing guidance

Test this demo with keyboard-only input, VoiceOver, and NVDA. The visible focus indicator and status message make the result easy to inspect.
