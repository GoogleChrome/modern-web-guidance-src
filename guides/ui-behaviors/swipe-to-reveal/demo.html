3. Swipe-to-reveal side menu on a scrollable pane

The outer horizontal wrapper (.SwipeReveal) uses overscroll-behavior: chain so scroll chains into the parent scroller (the document) without the menu bouncing, while the inner content pane keeps the default auto behavior so its own vertical bounce still happens.

Scroll up and down inside this pane, or swipe from the right to reveal the contextual side menu.

The inner content container (.SwipeReveal-content.is-scrollable) keeps the default overscroll-behavior: auto so it retains its normal bounce effect and chains horizontal swipes out to .SwipeReveal to reveal the menu.

The outer horizontal wrapper (.SwipeReveal) sets overscroll-behavior: chain so that once the menu reaches its scroll boundary, excess scroll chains into the parent scroller (here, the document) without the menu bouncing.

In browsers that do not yet support the chain keyword, the property falls back to its initial auto value as a progressive enhancement, preserving scroll chaining across all engines.
