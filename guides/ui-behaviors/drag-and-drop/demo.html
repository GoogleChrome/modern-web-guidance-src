Tasks

Four layouts driven by the same reordering code; the container's classes select scrolling, axis, and wrapping. A real implementation ships one keyboard mechanism per list.

Vertical, scrollable, grabbable handle

Drag an item with its handle, or press Space on a focused handle to grab it, move it with ArrowUp or ArrowDown, then press Space to drop or Escape to cancel. Dragging past the list's edge scrolls it.

Vertical, move buttons

Drag an item by its handle, or use its Move up and Move down buttons. The handle is pointer-only.

Horizontal, scrollable, grabbable handle

Same as the first list, along the inline axis: ArrowLeft and ArrowRight move a grabbed item, and dragging past the list's edge scrolls it.

Grid, grabbable handle

Items wrap into rows. ArrowLeft and ArrowRight move a grabbed item one place in reading order; ArrowUp and ArrowDown move it one row.
