Three ways to reorder, shown separately because a real implementation picks one keyboard interaction. Every move is announced in one live region at the bottom.

1. Drag handle, vertical

Drag an item with its handle, or press Space on a focused handle to grab it, move it with ArrowUp or ArrowDown, then press Space to drop or Esc to cancel.

2. Move buttons, vertical

No handle and no dragging: visible buttons move an item up or down. This is the most discoverable option and works in screen-reader browse modes.

3. Drag handle, horizontal

The same code along the inline axis. Drag a card, or grab it with Space and move it with ArrowLeft or ArrowRight. The arrows follow the visual direction, so they flip in right-to-left layouts.
